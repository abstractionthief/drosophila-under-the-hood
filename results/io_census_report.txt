Discrepancies vs. initial conceptual categories:
  1. "~166k neurons" matches status == 'Traced' (165122), NOT the raw census
     row count (211579). The raw census also includes Glia, Orphan, Assign,
     Anchor, and Unimportant bodies, almost none of which have a superclass.
  2. `class` is essentially unusable for output classification: among the
     1019 rows with a motor/endocrine/efferent superclass, `class` is NA for
     1019 of them (100%). Deriving output_category will require exitNerve /
     somaNeuromere / type-name patterns instead, per docs/io_classification_rules.md.
  3. `receptorType` covers only 752 (0.4%) of all rows - far too sparse to use as a
     general classification field; useful only for specific OSN drill-downs.
  4. Several superclasses carry an explicit '_tbc' (to-be-confirmed) suffix
     in the dataset itself; these are treated as unknown rather than forced
     into a role, honoring the dataset's own uncertainty rather than ours.
  5. The candidate input_category 'auditory_antennal' has no direct match in
     `class`; only `subclass` hints exist (e.g. 'auditory', 'wind_gravity',
     'chordotonal organ') and will need dedicated subclass-level rules.
  6. exitNerve -> body-part mapping (leg/wing/haltere/etc.) is not provided
     by any MaleCNS field or package dataset inspected so far; any such
     mapping relies on external published MANC nerve nomenclature and must
     be flagged lower-confidence until cross-checked against the primary
     literature (see docs/io_classification_rules.md).
